## 1. Overview

This report summarizes the latest progress and findings from the PCS Database project, including comprehensive analysis of Personal Comfort Systems (PCS) effects on human thermal comfort.

---

## Data Analysis

### Data Preparation

The dataset used for this analysis contains measurements of various personal comfort systems at controlled ambient temperature conditions (25°C ± 1°C). The data includes:

- 21 different PCS devices (ID 1-21)
- Multiple intensity levels (PCS_Level: 0.0 to 1.0)
- Equivalent temperature measurements for whole body and individual body parts
- Angle-specific data for directional devices (180°, 225°, 270°)
